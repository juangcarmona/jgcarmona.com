## Rule

The site describes the work done for Plain Concepts clients without naming those clients.

## Rationale

The engagements are confidential to the employer and its clients.

## Examples

Forward Deployed Engineer work is described as AI-SDLC, training, audits, assessments and SRE.

## Exceptions

A client that publicly announces the collaboration may be named once the author approves it.

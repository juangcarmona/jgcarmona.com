## Intended Outcome

The reader learns something and knows where to find more.

## Entry Conditions

The reader has a link or a search result.

## Journey Narrative

The reader lands on an article, reads it without banners or tracking, and may continue to related topics, the author's projects or talks.

## Variants and Branches

The reader starts at the home page or search instead of an article.

## Completion Conditions

The reader finished the article or moved to another one.

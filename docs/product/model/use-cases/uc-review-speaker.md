## Goal

The organiser decides whether to invite Juan G. Carmona and knows how to do it.

## Trigger

The organiser opens the talks page.

## Preconditions

At least one sourced appearance exists.

## Main Flow

1. The organiser reads past appearances with their event, date, place and available video or slides.
2. The organiser reads the sessions on offer.
3. The organiser follows the invitation link to Sessionize or writes by email.

## Alternative Flows

The organiser checks projects or the about page before inviting.

## Failure Conditions

None.

## Postconditions

The organiser has a verifiable record and a contact route.

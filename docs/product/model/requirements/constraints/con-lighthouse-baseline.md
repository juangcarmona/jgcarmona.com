## Constraint

The repository follows the Lighthouse baseline and its visual-theme profile, with ProductShape for product intent and ArcShape for architecture.

## Rationale

The author's own sites share one delivery, design and documentation baseline.

## Consequences

Design changes use Lighthouse UI tokens; product and architecture changes go through their documents.

## Purpose

Engineers, architects and technical leaders who come to learn from articles in English or Spanish.

## Goals

Find a useful article quickly, read it comfortably on any device and follow related work.

## Responsibilities

Navigate, read, search, subscribe by RSS and optionally comment.

## Boundaries

Does not publish content and is never asked for consent, an account or personal data to read.

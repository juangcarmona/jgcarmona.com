## Purpose

Crawlers and language-model agents that index or read the site on behalf of people.

## Goals

Resolve one canonical URL per article and understand who wrote it.

## Responsibilities

Read the sitemap, structured data, RSS and llms.txt.

## Boundaries

Receives no private content; the site exposes the same facts to people and machines.

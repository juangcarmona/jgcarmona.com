## Rule

A talk, role, client or achievement is published only when the author provided it or a public source confirms it, and the source is recorded next to the data.

## Rationale

The site is evidence of the author's work; an invented title or event damages its credibility.

## Examples

The CONVEX 2026 talk title comes from the published recording; the RootedCON 2026 title from the author.

## Exceptions

None.

## Problem

jgcarmona.com has no written product definition. Decisions about URLs, translations, privacy, sourcing and confidentiality lived only in conversations and code, and several had drifted: duplicate article URLs, invented talk details and an untranslated copy of every post.

## Intended Product Outcome

The definition states who the site serves (readers, organisers, the author and crawlers), the three journeys it supports, the rules it never breaks (one canonical URL per article, real translations only, no cookies, sourced facts, no client names), its quality bars and the constraints it lives under.

## Rationale

The site is being rebuilt on the Lighthouse baseline; writing the rules down now lets future changes cite them instead of rediscovering them.

## Affected Product Areas

Reading articles, evaluating the author as a speaker or collaborator, and publishing.

## Open Questions

None.

## Product Acceptance

The author recognises every rule as a decision he made, and the built site satisfies each quality requirement.

## Out of Scope

Content curation of older articles, the tag taxonomy and image optimisation.
